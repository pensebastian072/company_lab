## The failure catalogue, and the rule each one buys

Every row is something that actually happened here and cost a re-run. The rule is the
cheapest thing that would have caught it.

| # | what happened | measured cost | rule from now on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | E61 shipped 25 Industrials objects that concluded nothing | 20 retired, sector blocked | **Gate at ingest, not after.** `batch_health.enforce` runs in the same command as ingest. Already code. |
| 2 | E57 assigned citations to fields mechanically | 11 objects retired, 163 companies frozen | **One claim, one field, read by a human or a model that quotes back the sentence.** A keyword hit is not a citation. |
| 3 | Abstention collapse: 4 rank-order fields 92% → 0% over 8 batches | 45 of 100 points | **Never quote a streak. State both errors symmetrically.** `CODEX_STANDARD_RULES.md`, enforced by `test_codex_prompt_rules.py`. |
| 4 | `market_share_direction` asked of a web researcher 85 times while `marketshare.py` sat unwired | 85 wasted field-searches | **A module without a production caller is not done.** Before any batch, `grep` the new module for an importer outside its test. |
| 5 | E79 built 64 objects from a local retrieval script | 36 concluded nothing, 100% single-domain, 44 of 72 claim texts one template | **Every object needs ≥1 non-issuer source** (REGULATOR, TRADE_PRESS, ACADEMIC or PRIMARY_DATA). An object evidenced only by its own members' filings is rejected at ingest. |
| 6 | Taxonomy split 20 units → 64, 26 of them singletons | 36 retirements, 131 companies stranded | **No split without evidence in hand.** A unit may be split only if the researcher can already produce one ordinal-supporting claim for EACH resulting unit. Splitting then failing to evidence is strictly worse than not splitting: the gate deletes the pieces. |
| 7 | Singletons created to isolate hard companies (DD alone; HON+MMM) | 5 of 9 final retirements were 1-company units | **A singleton must name its private or foreign peers in the object**, or the company folds into the nearest real unit. |
| 8 | `VERIFIED_LOCAL` certified vocabulary, not quotation | a third of 6,966 claims; 146 unlocatable at all | **Exact containment is the bar for new claims.** The fallback may warn, never certify. Fix `check_quote` first (below). |
| 9 | Batch 3 staged in the evening, dead at NY midnight — twice | two lost stagings | **Stage the request the same NY day it will be finalized.** Already in repo CLAUDE.md. |
| 10 | Roadmap listed closed items as open; object counts written from estimate (27 vs 28, 14 vs 16) | a prompt would have under-built two sectors | **A claim about state carries the query that produced it.** No count in a doc without the command beside it. |
| 11 | A citation URL was constructed rather than read (mine) | caught by verification | **A citation is data to be read, never a string to be assembled.** |
| 12 | `structural_growth` unanswerable from filings; 26 of the 146 unlocatable claims are this field | E79 got 3 of 64 | **Growth comes from a publishing body or it stays UNKNOWN.** Trade association, regulator or statistical agency. Never company revenue, employment, or a sentiment index. |

Two of these — 6 and 12 — are the ones that keep making people scratch their heads, because
both look like progress while they happen. A taxonomy split feels like precision; a proxy
figure feels like an answer. Both fail only later, at the gate.

## What changes mechanically, before the next batch

Three things, all cheap, all in the Claude lane:

1. **Fix `check_quote`** — `html.unescape`, map Unicode quotes/dashes/spaces to ASCII,
   NFKC-normalise, then compare; second pass ignoring whitespace for table-derived text.
   Measured yield: exact containment **66.1% → 97.9%**. Then the 0.6 overlap fallback
   becomes a warning, not a pass. (`verify.py` is Chat A's file.)
2. **One acceptance command per batch.** `batch_health` + `constant_fields` + field-fill
   versus the previous batch + source-diversity + exact-containment rate, one exit code.
   A batch that regresses any of them is rejected **before** ingest, not audited after.
3. **Re-fetch or demote the 146.** They are tagged; the tag is not a resolution.

## The two lanes

**Codex — research.** Phase A objects and Phase B company batches. Never applies a gate,
never scores, never writes the store. Returns artifacts and a report.

**Claude — instrument and product.** Verification, gates, taxonomy, ingest, scoring,
workbook, audits. Never launders a research task it could do itself into the other lane.

Contention rule unchanged: one request file, B holds it, announce before taking it. Phase A
needs no request file, so a Phase A round can always run in parallel with a Phase B batch.

## What this plan does not claim

It does not claim the ranking predicts returns. E05 measured the top decile at −7.6% median
3-year excess against SPY on a survivorship-free holdout, and E03 measured 95.6% of ranking
power as sector selection. Coverage buys a more honest instrument and a book a human can
read — it does not buy evidence that the score works, and no amount of finished sectors
will. That test is the forward-return grader, and it needs snapshots we are still
accumulating.
