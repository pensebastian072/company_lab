# B8 - the E02 pairing, and the verdict that moves with it

Run 2026-09-10. Advisory / SHADOW; `promoted` is false. **No verdict is changed here** and
no number in either file was edited. Both files were labelled in place.

## What B8 said, and what it actually is

The 2026-09-07 backcheck raised it as a labelling problem: *"E02 results file is a later
rerun while its addendum discusses the original. Pair the versions or label them."* The
2026-09-09 reproduction confirmed it from file timestamps and judged that *"the numbers
themselves are not in question."*

The numbers are not in question. **The verdict is.**

## The two runs

| | `E02_addendum.md` | `E02_results.md` / `.json` |
|---|---|---|
| written / run | 2026-08-10 18:31 | 2026-08-12 10:48 |
| panel | 57,610 obs, **495 companies** | 67,889 month-ends, **675 companies** |
| window | 2016-08 to 2026-08 | 2016-01-31 to 2026-08-31 |
| Q1 top decile beats bottom | **3 of 10 years** | **6 of 10 years** |
| Q1 mean monthly IC | **+0.013** | **+0.043** |
| Q1 recorded verdict | **FAIL** | **`passes: true`** |
| Q3 bad rate | 11.1% vs 9.8% base rate | 13.93% |

The addendum's scoreboard opens with *"Q1 measured score ranks forward returns - **FAIL**"*.
The results file the reader has in front of it records that same question as passing, on a
universe 36% larger. Anyone reading the pair takes away the opposite conclusion on the
study's first question depending on which file they open.

This is why a broken pairing is not a cosmetic defect. A stale interpretation attached to
fresh numbers is indistinguishable from a current one.

## The reproducibility gap nobody flagged

Checked from the parquet files rather than from either document:

| file on disk | rows | companies | which run |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `E02_panel.parquet` | 57,610 | 495 | the **addendum's** |
| `E02b_panel.parquet` | 53,486 | 483 | a third panel |

**The 2026-08-12 run's panel was never saved.** The file named `E02_panel.parquet` is the
older one, so anything rebuilt from the panel on disk reproduces the addendum's numbers
and not the results file's - silently, and with the right-looking filename.

That is a worse failure than the pairing itself. The pairing can be fixed by labelling;
this cannot, without a re-run.

## What was done

Both files now carry a dated header note naming the run they describe, the other run's
figures, and the divergence. Neither number was edited and neither verdict was picked.

## What is still open - a decision, not a task

**Which E02 run is canonical?** The candidates are not equal in the way they look:

- The 08-12 run is newer and broader (675 vs 495 companies), which normally wins.
- But its panel is gone, so it is the one that cannot be reproduced or audited.
- And the 495-company panel that survives is the one the addendum's critical reading was
  written against - the reading that says FAIL.

A defensible resolution is to **re-run E02 on a saved panel and pair a fresh addendum to
it**, rather than declaring either surviving artifact canonical. Until then, quoting E02's
Q1 result requires naming the run, and the two answers are +0.013 / 3-of-10 / FAIL and
+0.043 / 6-of-10 / pass.

**This does not rescue or condemn the measured half.** E05's top decile still ran -7.6%
median three-year excess against SPY on a survivorship-free holdout, and E03 still puts
95.6% of the ranking's power in sector selection. Those are separate studies on separate
panels and nothing here touches them.

## What this does and does not establish

It establishes that the two E02 artifacts describe different populations, that they
disagree on Q1's verdict rather than only on its digits, and that the surviving panel
belongs to the older run.

It establishes nothing about which answer is right. Both runs measured what they measured;
only one of them can still be checked.
